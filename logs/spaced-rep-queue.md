# Spaced-Repetition Queue

Re-attempt each solved problem at **+14 days** and **+30 days** from the first solve. Outcome: `clean` / `with-assist` / `forgot`.

**Mode rule:** re-attempt on/before the due date → **INTERVIEW** (real retention test). Slipped past due → **TEACH** (relearn cleanly; a slipped INTERVIEW gives noisy data).
